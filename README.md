## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **The World Well: Blood of Caro**. This trainer enhances the game's story-focused systems by helping players manage choice progression, unlock alternate outcomes, access character outfits, and replay important narrative routes without changing the core visual novel experience.

**Target Audience:** Suitable for players who want to explore every dialogue branch, unlock all endings and outfits, replay the approximately 12 to 14 hour story more efficiently, or experience the narrative without repeatedly rebuilding the same route progress.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

The game uses point-and-click interaction and visual novel navigation rather than free-form character movement. Use the mouse or supported controller controls to advance scenes, select dialogue, inspect available points of interest, and continue through chapters.

### Tools & Inventory

There is no traditional item inventory or equipment system. Progress is primarily controlled through dialogue decisions, narrative flags, chapter states, character relationships, and unlockable appearances.

### World & Environment

The story takes place in a dystopian dark fantasy world ruled by vampires, where magic and advanced technology coexist. Follow 4 and Noma across kingdoms affected by war, uncover hidden motives, and use branching decisions to reveal different perspectives and story outcomes.

### Combat & Weapons

The game does not use a conventional combat system with real-time attacks, health bars, weapons, or tactical battles. Conflict is presented through cinematic storytelling, dialogue, investigation, and consequential decisions. Trainer options therefore focus on narrative progression rather than combat statistics.
